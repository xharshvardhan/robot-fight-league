Fight Gym Plan

Why the fights look unstable and reactive, and a different way to train that fixes the causes. Proposal for review, 13 Nov 2026. Nothing here is built yet.

Your diagnosis is right, and the causes can be named. The fighter was taught to hit one target, right now, any way it can. Nothing in its training rewards being balanced after the hit or setting up the next one.

Proposal: stop stitching separate skills together with a rule. Train one fighter in one gym where it sees several targets at once, chooses its own limb and order, and is scored on the whole exchange. Your multi-target idea is the first stage of it.

3. Training in four stages

Each stage starts from the previous stage's network. The scorecard (head 3, body 2, legs 1, by force) is the same from stage 1 to stage 4, so the fighter never has to relearn what counts.

5. Faster feedback loop

Today one idea is tried at a time on one small GPU and takes about 3 hours before you can watch anything. Three changes:

Try 6 to 8 ideas at once. The project allows 8 small GPUs, 16 mid-size and 16 large in this region; we have been using 2. Each feedback round launches several variants of the reward side by side instead of one after another.
Screen in 25 minutes. Every variant first runs a short trial and is scored on the same fixed test (points, falls, time in clinch, stance). Only the best two or three run to the end. Weak ideas die early.
Clips arrive by themselves. When a run ends it scores itself, records its fight clips, adds them to the clips page and shuts its machine down. You open one page and compare variants side by side.

I would also test a mid-size GPU against the small one on the same job before committing; I expect roughly twice the speed but have not measured it.
